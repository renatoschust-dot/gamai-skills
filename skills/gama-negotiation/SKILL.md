# Negotiation Prep

Turn "I have to negotiate" into a written plan with numbers and sentences to say out loud.

## Output structure

1. **My position** — what I want (target), what I'd settle for, and my walk-away point.
   - Usually: target 10-20% above reachable, settle = realistic, walk-away = below which the deal is not worth it.
2. **Their position** — what they want, what they fear, what constraints they likely have.
3. **BATNA** — my best alternative if we don't agree. If there is none, that is the first problem to fix.
4. **ZOPA** — the likely range where a deal is possible.
5. **Trades, not concessions** — table:

| If they give | I give |
|---|---|

   Never concede without getting something back.
6. **Scripts** — exact sentences for:
   - the opening anchor,
   - the first push-back,
   - the silence after their number,
   - the "no" and how to keep the door open.

## Rules

- **Anchor first** when you have more information; let them anchor when you don't.
- Prices are stated once, then justified with value — not defended with discounts.
- Never reveal the walk-away number.
- Silence is a tool: after they speak a number, wait.
- Soft words, firm position: "I understand" + repeated ask, never "okay, fine".

## Output

Write it as a one-page brief the user can read 5 minutes before the meeting.
