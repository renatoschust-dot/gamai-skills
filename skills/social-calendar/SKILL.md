# 30-Day Social Calendar

A month of posts a small business can actually execute. Variety fixed, effort capped.

## The mix (per week)

- **1 educational** — teaches something in the niche.
- **1 behind-the-scenes** — how the work is done.
- **1 proof** — before/after, testimonial, number.
- **1 offer** — product/service, soft.
- **1 personal/opinion** — builds trust.
- **2 free slots** — trending/reply/seasonal.

## Output format

| Day | Date | Platform | Format | Idea (1 line) | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rules

- Every post must fit the business's niche and audience — ask once who the audience is.
- The idea line must be concrete enough to shoot/write in 20 minutes.
- No filler posts ("Good morning!").
- Vary formats: photo, carousel, short video, text, story poll.
- Batch by production type (all photos one day, all videos one day).
- If there is nothing real to post that day, mark it `FREE / odgovori na komentare` — never invent fake results.

## Bonus

After the calendar, list **5 evergreen posts** that can be reused every month with a changed photo.
