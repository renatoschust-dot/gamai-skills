# Social Caption

One post, platform-native, no hashtag spam.

## Platform rules

| Platform | Length | Tone | Hashtags |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook | 40-80 words | conversational, story-first | 0-3 |
| Instagram | 80-150 words | visual, short lines, emoji ok | 5-8 niche, not generic |
| LinkedIn | 120-200 words | insight-first, no emoji spam | 3-5 |
| TikTok | 15-40 words | hook in first 3 words, casual | 3-5 |

## Structure

1. **Hook** (first line) — the only line that decides if anyone reads on. A number, a question, a contradiction, or a result.
2. **Body** — 1 idea only. Concrete: a number, a before/after, a specific detail.
3. **CTA** — one action (comment a word, tap the link, DM, save). Never three.

## Rules

- No: "Unlock", "Game-changer", "Elevate", "In today's fast-paced world", "Let's dive in".
- First person > brand voice ("we tested" beats "brands should").
- One emoji per ~50 words max, and only if it adds meaning.
- Give 2-3 hook variants to A/B test.
- If the user is in BS/HR/SR, write in that language, ijekavica by default.

## Output

```
PLATFORM: <name>
HOOK A: ...
HOOK B: ...
POST:
<body>
HASHTAGS: ...
```
