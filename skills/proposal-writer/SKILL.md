# Proposal / Scope of Work

One document, structured so a client can say **yes** without a second meeting.

## Structure

1. **Their problem** (not your bio) — 3-5 lines showing you understood the brief. If you can't state the problem, the proposal is not ready.
2. **Approach** — how you'll solve it, in phases. Name the *method*, not the tool list.
3. **Deliverables** — a bullet list of exactly what the client receives. Be specific (`3 landing pages`, `1 report`, `code repo`).
4. **Out of scope** — what is explicitly NOT included. This line prevents 80% of disputes.
5. **Timeline** — phases with dates or working days, and what you need from the client at each step.
6. **Price** — table: item | qty | price | total. State currency, VAT, payment schedule (e.g. 50/50).
7. **Why you** — 3 concrete proofs (past result, similar job, number). No adjectives.
8. **Next step** — one action: sign, pay deposit, or a 15-min call.

## Rules

- Lead with **their** outcome, not your company history.
- Never invent case studies, numbers, or client names — use `[primjer]` placeholders.
- Every deliverable must be verifiable ("what does 'done' look like?").
- Price is stated once; if the budget is unknown, give a range and say what drives it.
- Keep it to 1-2 pages. Anything longer gets skimmed.
