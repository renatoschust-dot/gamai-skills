# Job Description & Interview Kit

Two parts: an ad that gets the right people to apply, and questions that separate them.

## Part 1 — Job ad

1. **Title** — plain, searchable (no "Ninja", no "Rockstar").
2. **What the person will actually do** — 5-7 concrete responsibilities, in verbs.
3. **Must-have** — 3-5 requirements. Everything else goes to "nice to have".
4. **What we offer** — salary range (if the user allows), schedule, location/remote, growth.
5. **How to apply** — one clear step, plus **one test task** that filters the pool.

## Part 2 — Interview kit

- **5 screening questions** (phone/chat): quick filters, each with what a good answer sounds like.
- **6 interview questions** — behavioral ("tell me about a time…"), each with green/red flags.
- **1 practical task** — the closest thing to the real work, time-boxed.
- **Scoring rubric** — 1-5 per key competency, so two interviewers compare fairly.

## Rules

- Never invent a salary or perks — use `[raspon]` if not given.
- No discriminatory requirements (age, gender, marital status) unless legally required for the role.
- The test task must be doable in < 60 minutes.
- State the red flag for each interview question — that is what makes the kit usable.
